How to Calculate Stackable Black Friday Coupons Step by Step

Learning how to calculate stackable Black Friday coupons is simple once you know that most stores apply discounts one after another instead of adding percentages together. For example, if an item is $200 with a 30% sale and an extra 15% coupon:

  • First discount: 30% off $200 takes off $60, leaving $140.
  • Second coupon: 15% off the remaining $140 takes off $21, leaving $119 before tax.
  • Sales tax & cashback: Sales tax is added on top of the discounted price in most states, and any card cashback lowers your final cost after checkout.
