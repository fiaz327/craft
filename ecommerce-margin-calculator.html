How to Use This Profit Margin Calculator for Online Sales

When you sell products online, it is easy to think a sale made good money until you count every cost. A product profit calculator helps you see what is actually left in your pocket after you pay for the item, shipping, box or mailer, store fees, tax, and ads.

Start with your selling price and what you paid for the product. Then add your shipping and packing cost so nothing is left out. Whether you sell on your own store, do dropshipping, or list items on places like TikTok Shop, Etsy, eBay, or Amazon, every selling channel has its own fee rules. That is why you can type your own platform fee percent, card or payment fee percent, and any flat fee per order.

If you run ads or pay for promotions on an order, put that cost in too. Right away, you will see your real profit per order and profit margin in plain numbers. If your ad cost is too high, you will spot it early before losing money on more orders, and you can check the suggested selling price to hit the margin you want.
