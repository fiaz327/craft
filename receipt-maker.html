Small Business Billing & POS Guide

How to Make a Printable Shop Receipt Online Without Accounting Software

If you run a small grocery shop, bakery, clothing store, or repair counter, giving a neat receipt to your customer always build good trust. You don't really need to buy expensive POS software or pay monthly fees just for making bills. With our free online receipt maker, you can easily create a clean, printable shop receipt or customer invoice right inside your browser in just few seconds.

1 Flexible Unit & Weight Pricing Made Simple

Retail stores rarely sell everything by exact unit count. When adding products to your customer receipt, you can easily select between Units, Kilograms (kg), Grams, Boxes, or Liters. Switch between per-unit pricing (which auto-multiplies by quantity) and overall flat pricing for pre-weighted or bundled items.

2 Automatic Balance Tracking & Status Stamps

Record cash, card, or mobile wallet payments to calculate remaining customer balances instantly. When an invoice is paid in full, toggle our official stamp feature to apply a crisp green PAID seal, or a red PENDING stamp if a balance remains due.
