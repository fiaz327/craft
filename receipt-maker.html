Small Business Billing Guide

How to Make a Printable Shop Receipt Online Without Accounting Software

Whether you run a neighborhood grocery store, bakery, repair counter, or freelance service desk, handing a clear itemized receipt builds immediate trust with your customers. Instead of installing bulky invoicing apps or paying monthly fees, you can generate a clean, print-ready customer receipt right inside your browser in under a minute.

1 Flexible Unit & Weight Pricing Made Simple

Shops rarely sell everything the same way. When adding items to your receipt, choose between Units, Kilograms (kg), Grams, or Boxes. You can also switch the rate dropdown between Per Unit / Per Kg (which multiplies automatically by quantity) or Overall Price when charging a flat bundle rate for loose items.

2 Automatic Balance Tracking & Status Stamps

Enter the cash or card amount received from your customer to see the remaining balance update immediately. If the customer pays the full bill, our stamp engine places a crisp green circular PAID seal featuring your store name. If a partial payment leaves an unpaid balance, it automatically switches to a red PENDING stamp so both you and your client have clear records.

Save Your Store Header Locally for Faster Repeat Billing

Click Remember Shop at the top of the page to store your business name, phone number, and street address inside your browser's local storage. Next time you open the receipt maker on your phone or counter PC, your store header loads automatically—with zero cloud uploads and complete customer privacy.
